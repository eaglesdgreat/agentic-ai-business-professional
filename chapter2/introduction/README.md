# Introduction to Understanding Your Data

Most people picture a business running on one big spreadsheet. In reality, the information a company needs is spread across many separate tools, and those tools rarely talk to each other. Your sales team works in one system, operations in another, and customer service in a third. Before you can answer a question that crosses those boundaries, you have to bring the data together in a way you can trust.

This module is about that first step. You will learn why connecting data matters, what goes wrong when you skip the groundwork, and how a short planning habit makes every later dashboard and analysis more reliable. By the end, you will be able to explain the value of unified data and describe what belongs in a data source inventory before any files get connected.
