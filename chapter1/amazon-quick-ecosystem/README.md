# The Amazon Quick Ecosystem

Amazon Quick is an AI-powered service for analyzing data, automating work, building applications, and conducting research. You interact with it through natural-language chat, and it uses AI agents to work against the data and applications you connect. For this course, the part that matters most is its business intelligence side.

A quick note to clear up a common mix-up. Amazon Quick is not a general office or productivity service, the kind you use for email and documents. Its job is to connect your data, make sense of it, and help people act on it together.

Here are the parts you'll use in this course:

- ***Dashboards*** is the section of Amazon Quick where you build dashboards. It connects to data sources and turns prepared data into charts and interactive views people can read at a glance. This is the section you'll spend the most time in.
- ***Connecting your data*** — Amazon Quick connects your documents and data sources so that answers are grounded in your own information rather than generic knowledge.
- ***Chat agents*** are the natural-language interface. You ask questions of your data in plain language and get answers back, instead of building a new chart for every question.
- ***Spaces*** is where teams collaborate. A space gathers dashboards, data, and documentation into one shared hub, with permissions that control who can see and change what.

Amazon Quick also includes AI agents and research features. You'll meet those as the course connects your data to the questions people actually ask.

You'll work through these parts in roughly that order: connect and prepare data, visualize it, question it in natural language, and collaborate on it in a space.

Key terms
- ***Dashboards:*** the section of Amazon Quick where you build dashboards and visualizations, and the course's primary focus.
- ***Connecting your data:*** Amazon Quick connects documents and data so answers are grounded in your information.
- ***Chat agents:*** the natural-language interface for asking questions of your data.
- ***Spaces:*** shared team hubs for dashboards, data, and documentation.
