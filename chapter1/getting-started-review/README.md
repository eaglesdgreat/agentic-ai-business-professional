# Getting Started Review

Here are the key points to carry forward:

- **Amazon Quick** is an AI-powered service for analyzing data, automating work, building applications, and conducting research. It is not a general office productivity service.

- **Dashboards** is the section of Amazon Quick that connects to data sources and builds dashboards and visualizations. It's the course's primary focus.

- **Quick Index** connects your documents and data so answers from Quick are grounded in your own information.

- **Quick chat** is the natural-language interface for asking questions of your data.

- **Quick spaces** gathers a team's dashboards, data, and documentation into one shared, permissioned hub.
